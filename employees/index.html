Asking for access

  1. Make your own account

    At github.com/signup, with your own email address. You type your own password; nobody else ever sees it.

  2. Tell Jeff your username

    Only your username. Access is the business's to give: Jeff, or Christopher for him, sends the invitation.

  3. Accept the invitation

    It arrives by email, or under the bell icon on GitHub. Then open the members' reading room while signed in.

Nobody here will ever ask for

your password, a sign-in code or a recovery code. Not Jeff, not Christopher, not any helper. If someone does, the answer is no, and tell Jeff.
