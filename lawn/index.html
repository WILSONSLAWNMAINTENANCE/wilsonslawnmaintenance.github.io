What does my lawn need this month?

A rough calendar for lawns around Glen Rock and the rest of south-central Pennsylvania. Pick a month; this one is picked for you.

General, not advice. This is a rough guide for cool-season lawns (the fescues, bluegrass and ryegrass that most lawns here are made of). Weather moves it by weeks, and every yard is its own. For your yard, ask Jeff at (717) 825-8432, or read the free lawn guides from Penn State Extension. If you use any lawn product, follow its label.

November

Late fall

In the grass

  • Growth slows to a stop; the last mow of the year usually comes this month.
  • Get the last of the leaves off before the snow.

Around the yard

  • Stack firewood off the ground and under cover.

  • Firewood
